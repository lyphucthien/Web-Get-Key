Privacy

This page explains what data LPT Hub handles when you get and use a key. LPT Hub does not sell data and does not use its own analytics or advertising tools.

Data we store

  • IP address: stored for up to 3 hours to limit each device to one key every 3 hours.
  • Issued keys: stored so we can check whether a key is valid.
  • Device ID (HWID) and first-use time: sent by the LPT Hub script to tie a key to one device and calculate the 3-hour expiry.
  • Language: detected once from your IP address by Vercel to choose English or Vietnamese (only Vietnam gets Vietnamese). The IP address is not stored, and your choice is kept in your browser.
  • Minecraft username and Discord ID: only when you activate a key for the Minecraft server, used to confirm the account has been activated.

Cookies and browser storage

When you click a short link, the site sets a lpt_start cookie (up to 30 minutes) to check that you followed the proper flow, and a verify_token cookie (up to 15 minutes) to issue the key. The key you receive and the time you received it are saved in your browser (localStorage) so it can be shown again and the wait time can be counted down.

Third parties

The site is hosted on Vercel and key data is stored with Vercel KV. The short links (Work.ink, Link 4m, Link 4 Sub) are run by third parties with their own privacy and advertising policies that LPT Hub does not control.

Data deletion requests

If you want your Minecraft username, Discord ID or key removed from the system, message us on Discord as described on the Contact page.
