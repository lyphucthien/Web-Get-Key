Contact LPT Hub

LPT Hub's official support channel is its Discord server. You can ask how things work, report key problems, suggest new games, or request deletion of your data there: https://discord.gg/gyZ27Hw9qu. LPT Hub does not currently offer email or phone support.

When reporting a key problem

For faster help, tell us which link you chose (Work.ink, Link 4m or Link 4 Sub), when you did the link, and the message shown on the key page. A screenshot is usually enough. Do not share a key you are using with strangers.

Response time

LPT Hub is a personal project, so response times can vary. Before messaging, try again from the start and do not leave the short link halfway, because most problems come from leaving the link too early or going too fast.
